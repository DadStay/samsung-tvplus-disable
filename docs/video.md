## Follow the current written guide

Keep the [quick start](quickstart.md), [compatibility table](../README.md#compatibility)
and [recovery guide](recovery.md) alongside the film. Commands use placeholders;
replace them locally and keep your network information private. Native menus in
the film are labelled illustrations. Repository/release captures are public pages.

This disables unwanted playback and hides the Home icon through Samsung's native
menu. It is **not a complete uninstall**: a black channel title or disabled notice
can remain. The exact model/build guard is essential. One TV was tested with prior
changes; the factory-default recipe was not independently tested, and full live
rollback remains untested. The film is a demonstration, not new live-TV validation.
